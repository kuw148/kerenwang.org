I’m excited to share that I was recently featured on <a href="https://cod.ckcufm.com/programs/629/info.html"><strong>Free City Radio</strong></a> in an in-depth conversation about my research on the concept of <strong>human sacrifice in capitalism</strong>. The interview, now <a href="https://soundcloud.com/freecityradio/229-author-keren-wang-on-human-sacrifice-as-inherent-to-capitalism-today?utm_source=clipboard&amp;utm_medium=text&amp;utm_campaign=social_sharing"><strong>available on SoundCloud</strong></a>, is part of an interview series that examines the foundational realities of modern-day capitalism, specifically shaped by the notion of <strong>human sacrifice</strong> as a necessary element of economic systems.

<strong>Here’s a link to the interview: <em><a href="https://soundcloud.com/freecityradio/229-author-keren-wang-on-human-sacrifice-as-inherent-to-capitalism-today" rel="noopener" target="_new">Author Keren Wang on Human Sacrifice as Inherent to Capitalism Today</a></em></strong>. During this conversation, I explore how this framework, traditionally viewed through ancient rituals, continues in modern contexts through the exploitation of labor, environmental destruction, and systemic injustices.

The series is available as a podcast and will also air on the weekly <em><strong>Free City Radio</strong></em> program, which broadcasts on <a href="https://cod.ckcufm.com/programs/629/info.html"><strong>CKUT 90.3fm</strong></a> at 7pm across Canada. You can find the full archives of the program here: <strong><a href="https://soundcloud.com/freecityradio" rel="noopener" target="_new">Free City Radio on SoundCloud</a>.</strong>
<strong>Key Points Discussed:</strong>
<ul>
<li>How modern capitalism mirrors ancient rituals of human sacrifice.</li>
<li>The ways in which workers and marginalized communities are disproportionately affected by economic policies.</li>
<li>The intersection of <strong>rhetoric, law, and political economy</strong> in shaping contemporary forms of sacrifice.</li>
</ul>
